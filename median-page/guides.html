Start with one question.

How do I start exercising when I haven't exercised in years?

Start smaller than you think you need to. Walk, stretch, explore basic movements, and gradually increase the amount of activity your body can comfortably handle.

Do I need a gym?

No. A gym can be useful, but strength, mobility, endurance, and general movement can be developed in many different environments.

How often should I train?

There isn't one schedule that works for everyone. Your available time, experience, goals, recovery, and current fitness level all matter.

What should I do when I lose motivation?

Make the next action smaller. Put on your shoes. Walk for five minutes. Stretch while listening to a song. You don't have to feel motivated before you begin.

One last thing.

Your body isn't a project that needs fixing.

Take care of it. Challenge it. Rest it. Feed it. Let it take you places.
