# Surface Brief: plans.html

## Job and audience
Fitness enthusiasts choosing a structured plan and developers evaluating the Impeccable design skill. Both arrive to judge whether the plan options are clear, compelling, and worth committing to.

## Outcome and proof
Primary task: inspire visitors to choose a plan that fits their life and start their fitness journey. Success: visitors should immediately understand the three plan options, see which one matches their commitment level, and feel confident clicking "start." Real proof: the design demonstrates how the Nimbus design system adapts to a plans/pricing page while maintaining fitness product authenticity.

## Selected direction
Extend the established Nimbus dark world from move.html. Keep the deep dark background, electric lime accent, and three-typeface system. Use a card-based layout for the three plan options with clear visual hierarchy. Visual authority: the design system is already established; we adapt its components and palette for plans content.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
The page owns "plans without pressure" — fitness plans framed as starting points rather than obligations. The card layout establishes this immediately: each plan gets its own visual weight with clear commitment levels. The category-default arrangement of identical pricing cards is refused; each plan gets distinct treatment through size, accent color, and content hierarchy.

### OWN-WORLD
Nimbus dark world extended for plans: deep dark (#0d0f0e) background, surface (#151816) cards, electric lime (#c8ff3d) accent for primary CTAs. Space Grotesk for plan headlines, Inter for descriptions, IBM Plex Mono for duration labels. Cards with varied visual weight: 7-day Reset as entry point, 30-day Build as featured option, 12-week Adventure as premium. Layered elevation with color-matched glows on hover.

### STORY
Visitor arrives uncertain about committing to a fitness plan, leaves understanding there's a starting point for every commitment level. First impression: "These plans are designed for real life." Core belief: a plan should fit your life, not the other way around. Primary action: choose a plan and start moving.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
Hero section with headline "PLANS WITHOUT THE PRESSURE" in Space Grotesk, subtitle about choosing a direction. Three plan cards below: 7-day Reset (entry, smaller), 30-day Build (featured, larger with accent border), 12-week Adventure (premium, full-width). Each card has duration label, headline, description, feature list, and CTA. Background: deep dark with subtle gradient. Navigation: minimal, matching move.html.

### FORM
Persuade mode plans page. Hero section with headline and subtitle. Three plan cards in a responsive grid. "Build your own" section with feature grid. "What if you miss a day?" reassurance section. Footer with minimal links.

### FINISH
Unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Scope and boundaries
Fidelity: production-ready plans page. Breadth: single page (plans.html). Interactivity: basic hover states, focus states, simple animations. Target: plans.html file. What remains untouched: complete freedom to change everything. Anti-goals: avoid generic pricing page clichés, maintain the design system's integrity, don't pressure visitors.

## States and ranges
Content: hero section with headline, three plan cards with features, "build your own" section, reassurance section. States: default, hover, focus. Responsive: mobile, tablet, desktop.

## Interaction and layout
Hierarchy: hero → plan cards → build your own → reassurance → footer. Topology: hero section, responsive card grid, feature grid, single column for reassurance. Responsiveness: fluid columns, mobile-first. Affordances: clear CTAs, hover effects, focus indicators. Feedback: visual transitions on interaction.

## Constraints and open decisions
Platform: web. Delivery: static HTML/CSS. Accessibility: keyboard navigation, screen reader support. Localization: English only. Reusable components: adapt existing Nimbus components for plans context. Choices a builder must not invent: color palette, typography system, spacing scale.
