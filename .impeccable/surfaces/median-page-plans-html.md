# Surface Brief: plans.html

## Job and audience
Fitness enthusiasts choosing a structured plan and developers evaluating the Impeccable design skill. Both arrive to judge whether the plan options are clear, compelling, and worth committing to.

## Outcome and proof
Primary task: inspire visitors to choose a plan that fits their life and start their fitness journey. Success: visitors should immediately understand the three plan options, see which one matches their commitment level, and feel confident clicking "start." Real proof: the design demonstrates how a clean clinical light world adapts to a plans page while maintaining fitness product authenticity.

## Selected direction
Replace the Nimbus dark world with a clean clinical light world. Pure white background, light gray surfaces, cool blue accent. Same three-typeface system. Same content and structure — only visual treatment changes. Visual authority: clinical precision — the design communicates trust, clarity, and modern health-app quality.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
The page owns "plans without pressure" — fitness plans framed as starting points rather than obligations. The card layout establishes this immediately: each plan gets its own visual weight with clear commitment levels. The category-default arrangement of identical pricing cards is refused; each plan gets distinct treatment through size, accent color, and content hierarchy.

### OWN-WORLD
Clean clinical light world: pure white (#ffffff) background, light gray (#f8f9fa) card surfaces, cool blue (#2563eb) accent for primary CTAs and featured states. Space Grotesk for plan headlines, Inter for descriptions, IBM Plex Mono for duration labels. Cards with clean hairline borders, minimal shadows, and blue accent on the featured plan. No dark gradients, no glow effects — clarity through whitespace and contrast.

### STORY
Visitor arrives uncertain about committing to a fitness plan, leaves understanding there's a starting point for every commitment level. First impression: "These plans are designed for real life." Core belief: a plan should fit your life, not the other way around. Primary action: choose a plan and start moving.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
Hero section with headline "Choose a direction. Leave room for life." in Space Grotesk, subtitle about choosing a direction. Three plan cards below in a responsive grid: 7-day Reset (entry), 30-day Build (featured with blue accent border), 12-week Adventure (premium). Each card has duration label, headline, description, feature list, and CTA. Background: pure white with subtle light gray section breaks. Navigation: minimal, matching the clinical world.

### FORM
Persuade mode plans page. Hero section with headline and subtitle. Three plan cards in a responsive grid. "Build your own" section with feature grid. "What if you miss a day?" reassurance section. Footer with minimal links.

### FINISH
Unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Scope and boundaries
Fidelity: production-ready plans page. Breadth: single page (plans.html). Interactivity: basic hover states, focus states, simple animations. Target: plans.html file. What remains untouched: complete content and structural freedom — same copy, same sections, same order. Anti-goals: avoid generic pricing page clichés, maintain design system integrity, don't pressure visitors.

## States and ranges
Content: hero section with headline, three plan cards with features, "build your own" section, reassurance section. States: default, hover, focus. Responsive: mobile, tablet, desktop.

## Interaction and layout
Hierarchy: hero → plan cards → build your own → reassurance → footer. Topology: hero section, responsive card grid, feature grid, single column for reassurance. Responsiveness: fluid columns, mobile-first. Affordances: clear CTAs, hover effects, focus indicators. Feedback: visual transitions on interaction.

## Constraints and open decisions
Platform: web. Delivery: static HTML/CSS. Accessibility: keyboard navigation, screen reader support. Localization: English only. Reusable components: adapt existing components for light clinical context. Choices a builder must not invent: color palette, typography system, spacing scale.
